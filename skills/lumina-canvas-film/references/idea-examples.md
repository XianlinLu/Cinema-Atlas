# Idea examples

These examples demonstrate the reasoning pattern only. Adapt the subject and style to the user's actual words; do not reuse an example as a fixed template.

## A fragment about a place

**User thought:** “The old station near my house.”

**Possible direction:** Follow the station through one ordinary morning, using arriving and departing light to turn a place description into a story about change and memory.

**Style choice:** A restrained architectural line drawing with one warm accent can make the setting legible while giving the passing human moment emphasis.

## A fragment about a feeling

**User thought:** “I feel like I'm starting over.”

**Possible direction:** Show a small figure crossing from a crowded, tangled space into an open one, with a recurring line gradually becoming a path.

**Style choice:** Begin with compressed shapes and muted tones, then open the composition and palette; the visual change carries the emotion without explaining it in paragraphs.

## A topic request with no style

**User thought:** “Make a visual explainer about why leaves change color.”

**Possible direction:** Follow one leaf across the season and reveal the changing pigments as the central visual hook.

**Style choice:** A clean botanical illustration with labeled color layers balances a gentle story with factual clarity. Verify scientific details rather than inventing them.
