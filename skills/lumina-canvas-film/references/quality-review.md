# Quality review

Use this guide for factual work, accessibility, or a final canvas review.

## Story and factual integrity

- Each scene advances the idea; remove panels that only repeat it.
- Separate confirmed facts from metaphor, imagined scenes, and illustrative diagrams.
- Do not fabricate sources, measurements, quotations, events, or provenance.
- Put a source near the claim it supports when the canvas can represent it clearly.
- Keep uncertainty visible when a detail is unverified or approximate.

## Visual review

Check what the current tools let you inspect:

- Scene order is obvious and the opening leads to a meaningful ending.
- Text has sufficient size and contrast; captions do not compete with the main image.
- Alignment, spacing, and margins feel consistent across scenes.
- No important object, label, or connector is clipped or unintentionally covered.
- The style and recurring motif remain coherent from beginning to end.
- Placeholder, generated, or unverified material is distinguishable when that matters.

Fix specific issues and inspect again when possible. If the platform offers no inspection action, make no claim that a visual audit occurred.

## Accessible choices

Use contrast, labels, and shape or position differences in addition to color alone. Keep text concise and avoid relying on tiny text or color as the only way to communicate sequence or meaning.
